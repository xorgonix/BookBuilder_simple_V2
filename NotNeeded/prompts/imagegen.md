**Introduction**

Prompt engineering is the process of crafting text prompts that accurately convey your desired image output in AI-generated art. A well-crafted prompt can significantly improve the quality and accuracy of the generated image, while a poorly written prompt may lead to subpar results.

**The Positive Prompt**

A positive prompt contains all the details of what you want to see in your images (the subject), it also defines the medium, style, composition, and color & lighting of the image.

### Basic Prompt Elements

* **Subject**: The focal-point of our image – the main object we want to depict.
	+ Example: 1girl, woman, petite
* **Medium**: The materials or tools an artist uses to create their work. It can include things like oil paint, watercolors, charcoal, pencil, etc.
	+ Example: watercolor painting
* **Style**: Defines the artistic style of our image. Examples of style include impressionism, realism, pop-art, surrealism, etc.
	+ Example: impressionist background
* **Composition**: Describes how the various elements of the image are arranged within the artwork to create a pleasing result.
	+ Example: from above
* **Color & Lighting**: We have full control over both color and lighting in our images. This can include the color of particular items in our scene, or the overall hue.
	+ Example: rainbow hue

### Subject, Medium, Style, Composition, Color & Lighting

A well-crafted prompt should include all these elements to accurately convey your desired image output.

**Syntax and Structure**

It's good practice to apply a somewhat standardized syntax and structure when prompting. This includes:

* Breaking your prompt into sections
	+ The First Section: Subject & Setting
	+ The Second Section: Color, Style, and Lighting
	+ The Third Section: Composition & Additional Modifiers

**Formatting the Prompt**

Here's an example of how to format the prompt using these guidelines:

**Example Prompt:**

1girl, woman, petite, pale skin, detailed face, bobcut hair, blue eyes, wearing yellow tank top, happy, laugh, statement sunglasses in a park, sky, trees, moonlight, stars, vivid colors, bokeh background, dramatic color, cartoon, from below, cinematic, whimsical

This prompt includes:

* **Subject & Setting:** 1girl, woman, petite, pale skin, detailed face, bobcut hair, blue eyes, wearing yellow tank top, happy, laugh, statement sunglasses in a park, sky, trees, moonlight, stars
* **Color, Style, and Lighting:** vivid colors, bokeh background, dramatic color, cartoon
* **Composition & Additional Modifiers:** from below, cinematic, whimsical
